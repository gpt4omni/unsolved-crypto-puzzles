# Author posts and quotes

Short, dated material published by Bobby Lee (Ballet CEO, ex-CEO of BTCC) about this
challenge.

## 2020-07-24, X (Twitter)

https://x.com/bobbyclee/status/1289004702122643456

The original announcement of the challenge: three Ballet REAL cards, 1 BTC each,
released with one secret half exposed per card.

## Ballet's official rules (blog.ballet.com)

> "The tamper-evident sticker concealing the encrypted private key has been removed
> from one wallet, and the scratch-off concealing the passphrase has been removed
> from the other."

No specific passphrase characters or positions were ever revealed, and no further
hints were published after the original announcement.
