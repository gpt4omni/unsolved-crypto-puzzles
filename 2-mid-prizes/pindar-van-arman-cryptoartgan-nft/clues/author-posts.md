# Author material, dated

## The announcement, February 2022

Posted on the artist's own site, [bitgans.com/news/cryptoartgans](https://bitgans.com/news/cryptoartgans),
and restated on X on 2022-08-25 ([x.com/VanArman/status/1562799956175757313](https://x.com/VanArman/status/1562799956175757313)):

> "The last cryptoArtGAN disappeared into a mystery wallet."
>
> "The seed phrase is 11 glitches ending with a random word."
>
> "Everyone has been looking for glitches all wrong."

The artist adds that only one clue will ever be given for this puzzle, "even if it takes
over a year."

## A related, already-solved puzzle in the same body of work

On his journal page "014 - BIP39 and bitGANs," first posted 2022-01-01 and updated
2022-01-05 ([bitgans.com/journal/page014](https://bitgans.com/journal/page014)), the artist
writes about a different piece in the same wallet system:

> "Each has a BIP39 word in their essence property. [...] But I am beginning to wonder if
> their essence is just a distraction. I think they hide their true BIP39 nature elsewhere."
>
> "Their essence is just a distraction, they hide their true BIP39 nature elsewhere."

This already-solved puzzle establishes, in the artist's own words, that a printed attribute
column that looks like flavor text can itself be the encoded material, and that the obvious
reading (the "essence" property) can be a deliberate misdirection pointing to a different
column.

## The attribute table used in this folder

`data/bitgans-attributes.csv` is a transcription of a table published on the artist's
journal (bitgans.com/journal), covering multiple NFT series he has released under the same
wallet-puzzle system, including 8 rows belonging to the cryptoArtGAN / Act 1 series that
holds this puzzle's prize.
