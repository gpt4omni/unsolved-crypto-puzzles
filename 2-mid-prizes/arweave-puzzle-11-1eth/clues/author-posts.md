apps developer, and early investor), announced this puzzle on Twitter. The original tweet
was later deleted; the quotes below are reproduced from the community repository
`HomelessPhD/AR_Puzzles`, which mirrors them from Twitter replies at the time.

No further hint was ever posted in the community Telegram group `@arweavep`: I searched a full
local archive of that group (55,002 messages, November 2021 to May 2026) for any later message
announcing new hints and found none, and found 47 messages from other members independently
noting that the promised follow-up never arrived. Tiamat has no account in this Telegram group
at all (0 messages from that username); every quote of Tiamat inside it is itself a member's
copy-paste from Twitter or an earlier Discord, which is why this page cites Twitter and the
community repository directly rather than the Telegram archive.
