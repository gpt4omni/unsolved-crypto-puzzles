## No hint published since

Every other long-open puzzle in the same series (LTC, Codex, Demobit, Janus) eventually
received a published hint from the author. As of the most recent check of the author's site
(2026-07-02), HALV has none: probe URLs following the author's own naming pattern for hints
on other puzzles (for example `cryptoHALV_hint.svg`) return 404, and the site's page for HALV
is unchanged since the original 2024 announcement.
